# muse-confess1126b-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [Confess Your Worst Moment](https://make.fun/bounty/u-nayyy-confess-your-worst-moment-841ae7ef).

## The entry

> I practiced my resignation speech in the car for a week, then delivered it perfectly - to my boss's out-of-office voicemail, and accepted the counter-offer from the beep.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: a full story in one line - a week of rehearsal, a perfect delivery to the wrong audience, and surrender in the last four words, accepting a counter-offer from a beep.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt`.
